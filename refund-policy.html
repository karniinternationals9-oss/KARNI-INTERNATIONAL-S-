We want you to enjoy every cup. Because tea is a food product, we follow the rules below to keep things fair and hygienic for everyone.

1. Order cancellation

  • You can cancel your order before it is dispatched by contacting us on WhatsApp or at our email with your Order ID.
  • Once the order has been dispatched, it cannot be cancelled.
  • If we cancel your order for any reason (for example, stock not available or address not serviceable), you will receive a full refund.

2. Returns

For hygiene and food-safety reasons, opened or used food products cannot be returned. We will gladly help if:

  • You received a damaged, leaking or tampered pack.
  • You received the wrong product or wrong pack size.
  • Items are missing from your order.

Please contact us within 48 hours of delivery with your Order ID and clear photos (an unboxing video is very helpful). After checking, we will send a replacement or issue a refund.

3. Refunds

  • Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method through Razorpay.
  • Refunds are initiated within [2–3] business days of approval and usually reach your account within 5–7 business days, depending on your bank.
  • Shipping charges are refundable only when the order was cancelled by us or the product was damaged or wrong.

4. Failed payments

If money was deducted but your order was not confirmed, it is usually reversed automatically by your bank within 5–7 business days. If not, contact us with the payment details and we will help.
