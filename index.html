Brewing Guide

How to make the perfect chai

For 2 cups. Adjust milk, sugar and boiling time to your taste.

  1. Boil the waterBring 1 cup (about 200 ml) of water to a boil in a saucepan.
  2. Add Karni chai pattiAdd 2 heaped teaspoons of tea. Add crushed ginger or elaichi if you like.
  3. Let it simmerSimmer for 1–2 minutes until the colour turns deep and rich.
  4. Add milk & sugarPour in 1 cup of milk and sugar to taste. Bring to a boil twice.
  5. Strain & serveStrain into cups and enjoy – har chuski mein asli swaad!
