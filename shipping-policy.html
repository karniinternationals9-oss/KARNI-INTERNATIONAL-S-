1. Where we deliver

We deliver across India to pincodes serviced by our courier partners. If your pincode is not serviceable, we will contact you and refund the order in full.

2. Shipping charges

  • Free shipping on orders of ₹499 and above.
  • A flat shipping charge of ₹60 applies to orders below that amount.
  • The exact shipping charge is always shown at checkout before you pay.

3. Dispatch and delivery time

  • Orders are usually packed and dispatched within [1–3] business days after payment.
  • Delivery normally takes [3–7] business days after dispatch, depending on your location. Remote areas may take longer.
  • Orders are not dispatched on Sundays and public holidays.

4. Order tracking

Once your order is dispatched, we will share the courier name and tracking details on WhatsApp, SMS or email.

5. Delays

Delivery times are estimates. Delays can happen due to weather, festivals, strikes or courier issues. We will keep you informed and help you track your parcel.

6. Wrong address / failed delivery

Please make sure your address, pincode and mobile number are correct. If a parcel is returned because of a wrong address or the customer being unavailable, re-shipping charges may apply.

7. Damaged parcels

If the parcel looks damaged or tampered with at delivery, please take photos and contact us within 48 hours. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy.
