This Privacy Policy explains how Karni International's ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses and protects the information you share when you visit this website or buy from us. By using this website you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, mobile number, email address and delivery address when you place an order.
  • Payment details: payments are processed by Razorpay. We do not store your card, UPI or bank details on our website. Razorpay handles them as per its own privacy policy and applicable security standards.
  • Messages: details you share with us on WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Technical data: your cart is saved in your own browser (local storage). Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.

2. How we use your information

  • To process, pack, ship and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order, delivery or payment.
  • To respond to your questions and provide customer support.
  • To send offers or updates only if you have chosen to join (you can opt out any time).
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting requirements.

3. Sharing of information

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Payment gateway (Razorpay) to process your payment.
  • Courier and logistics partners to deliver your order.
  • Government authorities when required by law.

4. Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. All payments are made over secure, encrypted connections. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

5. Data retention

We keep order information for as long as needed to fulfil your order and to meet legal and tax requirements.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information (except where we must keep it by law) by contacting us at our email.

7. Cookies and local storage

This website uses your browser's local storage to remember the items in your cart. Third-party services such as Google Fonts, Google Maps and Razorpay may set their own cookies.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
